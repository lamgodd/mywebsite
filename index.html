Overview & Notes

Supplements vs Whole Foods:

  • Supplements cannot replicate all benefits of whole foods.
  • Whole foods provide fiber, antioxidants, phytochemicals, and other synergistic compounds.
  • Body absorbs and utilizes nutrients better from whole foods than from isolated supplements.

What is a Supplement? A supplement is an orally consumed product containing vitamins, minerals, herbs, amino acids, or other substances, intended to add nutritional value to the diet. Supplements may be natural or synthetic but are not whole foods.
