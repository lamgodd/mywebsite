📜 Quest Overview & Knowledge 📜

🍎 Whole Foods vs Supplement Potions:

  • Supplement potions cannot replicate all benefits of whole food items.
  • Whole foods provide fiber, antioxidants, phytochemicals, and other synergistic compounds like a well-balanced meal from the Gnome Restaurant.
  • Your character absorbs and utilizes nutrients better from whole foods than from isolated supplement potions.

⚗️ What is a Supplement Potion? A supplement is an orally consumed item containing vitamins, minerals, herbs, amino acids, or other substances, intended to add nutritional value to your diet. Supplements may be natural or synthetic but are not whole foods - think of them as stat-boosting potions for your IRL character.
