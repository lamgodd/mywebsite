Note: Supplements cannot replicate all benefits of whole foods. Whole foods provide complex nutritional matrices: fiber, antioxidants, phytochemicals, and other compounds working synergistically. Nutrient absorption and utilization differ in real food vs supplements. The body often absorbs and uses nutrients from whole foods better than isolated nutrients in supplements.

What is a Supplement? A supplement is a product taken orally that contains one or more ingredients (such as vitamins, minerals, herbs, amino acids, or other substances) intended to add nutritional value to the diet. Supplements are consumed to provide nutrients or beneficial compounds that might not be sufficiently obtained from food alone. They can be naturally occurring or synthetically produced, but they are not the same as whole foods.
